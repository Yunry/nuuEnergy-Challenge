Live demo: upload step by step

A customer workspace (data/demo/B-Live/) that starts with a stored questionnaire, home B-05 (2006, underfloor heating plus 8 radiators, gas), and no documents. A decision is already possible, but not certain: the radiators are unknown. Upload a radiator photo: the unclear one (07) changes nothing; the clear one (05) narrows the result to “possible”, green with the standard assumptions. Each upload is saved as a step with the file, the JSON read from it, the new result and a technician report.
