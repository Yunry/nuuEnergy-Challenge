Live demo: upload step by step

A customer workspace (data/demo/B-Live/) that starts with a short questionnaire, home B-03 (16 radiators, two-pipe, partly renovated; building age, area, bill and fuel left empty), and no documents: not enough to decide yet. Upload the energy certificate (PDF): it adds age, area, fuel and consumption, and the result becomes “possible”; the radiator type is still open. Then the radiator photo: it sets the radiator type and size, and the result turns green. Each upload is saved as a step with the file, the JSON read from it, the new result and a technician report.
