Live demo: upload step by step

A customer workspace (data/demo/B-Live/) that starts with the stored questionnaire (B-03’s answers) and no documents. Upload the energy certificate PDF: the result is possible but not yet certain, and it asks for a radiator photo. Then upload a radiator photo: a clear one (05) settles it and the result is approved; an unclear one (07) leaves it open. Each upload is saved as a step with the file, the JSON read from it, the new result and a technician report.
